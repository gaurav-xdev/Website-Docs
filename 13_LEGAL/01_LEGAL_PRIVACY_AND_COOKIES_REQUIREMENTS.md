# Legal Privacy and Cookies Requirements

## Documents
Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy where required, acceptable-use policy, refund/cancellation terms, security disclosure policy.

## Data inventory
Document identity data, billing metadata, account security data, developer data, download telemetry, analytics, support data, and retention.

## User rights
Provide legally applicable access, correction, deletion, export, objection/consent controls.

## Cookies
Essential cookies only by default. Non-essential analytics/marketing cookies require applicable consent.

## Processor/vendor register
Track payment, email, hosting, analytics, storage, monitoring, and other processors.

## Security
Legal documents must accurately describe architecture and limitations. Final language requires jurisdiction-specific legal review.

# SEO and Marketing Specification

## Technical SEO
Canonical URLs, sitemap, robots policy, semantic HTML, unique metadata, structured data only where accurate, Open Graph/Twitter cards, fast loading.

## Performance
Optimize LCP, CLS, INP. Lazy-load 3D/large assets. Preload only critical resources.

## Content
Use concrete capability explanations, real demos, security explanation, pricing clarity, developer documentation.

## Launch messaging
Early-access offer must use a server-authoritative eligibility count. Do not claim scarcity after the allocation is exhausted.

## Prohibited marketing shortcuts
No fake reviews, fake customer logos, fake download counts, fake uptime, fake benchmarks, or invented press.
